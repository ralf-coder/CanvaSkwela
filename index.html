    Privacy Policy

    Last updated October 25, 2026. CanvaSkwela is a student coursework project.

    What data is collected, and why

    There are no accounts, and your schedule is never sent to a server run by this project. The PDF you choose is read inside your browser, and only the class entries it finds (subject, day, time, and room) are kept; other text in the file, such as personal details, is discarded and never stored or sent. The subjects, days, times, and rooms you review or type, plus your display choices (title, theme, colors, layout), are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. They are saved only so your timetable is still there when you come back.

    How information is used, stored, and shared

    • Your data stays in your browser. It is not shared, sold, or used for advertising or analytics, and this app sets no cookies.
    • The first time you choose a PDF, your browser downloads the open-source PDF.js reader from the cdnjs content delivery network (operated by Cloudflare). That request can reveal your IP address and browser details to cdnjs, as any file request does. Your PDF is not sent there.
    • The website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) when delivering pages. This app can’t see those logs.

    Deleting your information

    Select to erase everything this app stored in your browser. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Because no copy exists anywhere else, there is nothing more to delete.

    Safeguards and limits

    Files are processed on your device, and saved data is only as private as your device and browser profile: anyone using the same profile can see it. Images, PDFs, and calendar files you download are saved wherever you choose. No system is perfectly secure, so please use test or non-sensitive schedules and avoid PDFs containing IDs, grades, or other personal records.
